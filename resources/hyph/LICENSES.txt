Hyphenation patterns in data/hyph

Kaia ships the TeX hyphenation patterns that KOReader ships for CREngine. The patterns come from the hyph-utf8 project, https://github.com/hyphenation/tex-hyphen, and KOReader's converted copies in base/thirdparty/kpvcrlib/crengine/cr3gui/data/hyph. The package does not change the pattern files.

CREngine loads only files whose names end in .pattern. This file and the other text in data/hyph are not read by the engine.
